On the recordings

Every clip here is a recording of an actual car, credited to whoever captured it — the credit sits on each card and links back to the source. Nothing on this site is synthesised. The rev control shifts a real recording rather than modelling an engine, which is why a 4.8-litre V10 still sounds like one at every point on the dial.

Sounds gathered from enthusiast channels and used here for non-commercial, editorial purposes. If you recorded one of these and would like it removed or credited differently, get in touch.

On the photography

Most photographs come from Wikimedia Commons under Creative Commons or public-domain licences, and each card names the photographer and the licence for the frame currently on screen. Hover a car to cycle through the rest.
